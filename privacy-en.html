MMA Career Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026 · App: MMA Career (Android: com.pocketagentgames.mmacareer, iOS: MMA Career on the App Store)

This policy explains what data the MMA Career mobile game (the "Game") processes, why, and how, in line with Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK") and the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

1. Controller and contact

  • Controller: Berke Can Çavuş (Pocket Agent Games)
  • Address: İhsaniye Mahallesi, İhsaniye Caddesi No: 62/1, Selçuklu/Konya, Türkiye
  • Email: pocketagentgame@gmail.com

2. Consent and choices

On first launch the Game shows a notice with equally prominent "I accept" and "I do not accept" options. If you decline, the Game is fully playable and ads, crash reporting and usage measurement are not started. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy; withdrawal does not affect processing before it. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form may also be shown.

3. Data we process

3.1 On-device game data. Career progress, settings, language, the fighter name and nickname you choose, referral codes used and photos picked in the editor stay on your device and are not sent to us. If Android's built-in Auto Backup is enabled, Google may back these up to your Google account according to your device settings.

3.2 Ads (only with consent). Rewarded ads are served by Google AdMob and mediation partners (myTarget; MY.GAMES/VK group, Russia), which may process your advertising ID, IP address, device model, OS, language and ad interaction data for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement and fraud prevention. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in device settings.

3.3 Crash reports (only with consent). Firebase Crashlytics collects stack traces, device model and maker, OS and app version, crash time and a random installation identifier to fix bugs.

3.4 Usage measurement (only with consent). Firebase Analytics records game events (screens, career start, tutorial steps, fight start and result, levels, in-game currency and UP changes, rewarded ad completion, store pack ID and price) with a random app instance ID. Advertising ID, Android ID, fighter name, referral codes and purchase tokens are not sent.

3.5 Google Play Games and cloud saves (only if you sign in). If you choose to sign in, the Game reads your Play Games player ID and name and stores your save in Google's Saved Games service, linked to your Google account.

3.6 Purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see card details. To prevent fraudulent or refunded purchases and grant the first-buyer gift, the purchase token, product ID and package name are sent to our verification server on Cloudflare Workers and checked with the Google Play Developer API. Tokens are not stored; for gift-winning purchases only the order ID, a SHA-256 hash of the token and the time are stored. Cloudflare may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses to operate the service.

3.7 Feedback (only if you send it). Your message plus game version, Android version, device model and language are sent to us via Google Forms. Please do not include personal details in your message.

3.8 Time check. To keep timed rewards fair, the Game sends an empty request to Google's connectivity check (connectivitycheck.gstatic.com), which exposes your IP address to Google.

3.9 Permissions. Camera/photos only when you pick a photo in the editor (it never leaves the device); notifications only for in-game reminders; internet for the services above.

3.10 Not collected. Name, email, phone, location, contacts, payment card, health or other special-category data. The Game has no user accounts.

4. Legal bases

Ads, crash reports, analytics and related international transfers: consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Arts. 5(1), 9). Purchase verification and fraud prevention: contract and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(b),(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c),(f)). Cloud saves and feedback: providing the service you request (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interests.

5. Recipients and international transfers

Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. (AdMob, Firebase, Play, Play Games, Forms) – EU, US and elsewhere; myTarget (MY.GAMES/VK group) – Russian Federation and elsewhere, for ad mediation on Android and iOS; Cloudflare, Inc. – global. Transfers rely on your consent, European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where applicable, and KVKK Art. 9 mechanisms. We do not sell personal data.

Transfer to the Russian Federation (myTarget): With your consent, on Android and iOS the myTarget ad network processes your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID; on iOS IDFA only with ATT permission, otherwise IDFV), IP address and coarse location derived from it, device model, OS, language and ad interaction data to bid on and show ads. As there is no adequacy decision or standard contractual safeguard for Russia under KVKK, and no EU adequacy decision, this transfer relies on your explicit consent (KVKK Art. 9 as amended by Law No. 7499; GDPR Art. 49(1)(a)). Possible risks include access by foreign authorities and difficulty exercising your rights. If you decline, no ads are shown and the Game plays the same.

6. Retention

On-device data: until you delete the Game or its data. Crashlytics: Google default (about 90 days). Analytics: the period set in Firebase (at most 14 months). Gift-winning purchase records: for the campaign and at most 2 years. Feedback: deleted once resolved, at most 2 years. Ad and Play data: per the providers' policies.

7. Your rights

You may request access, a copy, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and withdraw consent (GDPR Arts. 15–22; KVKK Art. 11) by emailing pocketagentgame@gmail.com. We reply free of charge within 30 days and may ask for details (e.g. feedback date, order ID) to find your data, since most data does not directly identify you. You may complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority or your local EU/UK supervisory authority. Self-service: withdraw consent in Settings, uninstall or clear app data, manage Play Games data in your Google account, reset your advertising ID.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we learn of such data we delete it; parents can contact us at the email above.

9. Security

Data is sent over TLS, no cleartext HTTP is used, and only minimal data is kept server-side with restricted access.

10. Changes

We update the effective date when this policy changes and announce material changes in the Game, asking for consent again where required.

11. iOS (App Store) version

  • Payments are processed by Apple's App Store; we never see card details. Any data sent to our server to verify purchases is limited to the same scope as on Android (transaction and product IDs).
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob and its mediation partner myTarget. Unless you grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, the Game does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If ATT permission is requested, it is only from the permissions section in Settings, at your request; revoke it in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Without ATT, ads may be non-personalised or limited, and AdMob may process the identifier for vendor (IDFV), IP address and device data for ad delivery and measurement.
  • Cloud saves, Game Center and iCloud are not used by the iOS version; saves stay on the device. If iCloud/device backup is on, Apple may back up app data.
  • Crash reports and usage measurement (Firebase) are currently not active in the iOS version; if enabled, they will run only with your consent and this policy will be updated.
  • Camera and photos: the photo picker (PHPicker) needs no permission; camera permission is asked only in the editor. Photos never leave the device.
  • Feedback includes your iOS version and device model instead of the Android version.
  • Apple processes App Store account data under its own Privacy Policy.
